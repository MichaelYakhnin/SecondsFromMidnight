FAQ

What are seconds from midnight?

Seconds from midnight represent the number of seconds elapsed since 00:00:00.


How are milliseconds calculated?

Milliseconds are simply seconds multiplied by 1,000.


Why use this calculator?

Developers often encounter timestamps stored as seconds or milliseconds from midnight in logs, PLC systems, embedded devices, SQL databases, industrial automation, telemetry systems, and APIs. This calculator makes converting those values quick and accurate.
